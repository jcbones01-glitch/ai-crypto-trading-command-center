# Legitimate Traders and Authors Who Teach Openly

**How they were checked:** each person needed a verifiable professional history (fund management, published books from established publishers, public track records, or contest results), and must teach risk honestly. Being on this list is **not** an endorsement of buying anything from them. Their books are often available free through libraries or apps like OverDrive/Libby.

## How to use them
- **Start with:** Rob Carver's blog "Start here" page, [Systematic trading: start here](https://qoppac.blogspot.com/p/systematic-trading-start-here.html). It's free.
- **Books, in order:** Carver *Systematic Trading* → Clenow *Following the Trend* → Chan *Quantitative Trading* → Davey *Building Winning Algorithmic Trading Systems*.
- **YouTube:** prefer long interviews with these people (for example podcast episodes on "Better System Trader" and "Top Traders Unplugged") over channels that show lifestyle, luxury, or "I made $X today" clips.
