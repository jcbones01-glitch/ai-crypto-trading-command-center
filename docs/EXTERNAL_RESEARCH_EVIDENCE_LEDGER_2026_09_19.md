This ledger does not certify profitability, validate any strategy, or authorize any empirical run.

## Evidence classification

- **Official** — regulator, exchange, central bank, BIS, IMF or similar primary institutional source.
- **Peer-reviewed / journal** — published academic review or empirical study.
- **Working paper / preprint** — useful but not treated as settled evidence.
- **Practitioner** — workflow/engineering insight only.
- **Community** — anecdotal implementation evidence only.

## Evidence-use rule

External evidence may:
- motivate a mechanism;
- identify a failure mode;
- suggest a baseline;
- inspire a preregistered experiment;
- improve governance;
- improve data-quality controls;
- improve execution assumptions.

External evidence may **not**:
- promote a strategy;
- substitute for project Validation/OOS;
- justify threshold relaxation;
- authorize protected-data access;
- justify paper/live trading.

Project-generated evidence remains controlling.
