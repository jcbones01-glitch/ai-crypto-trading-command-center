# Project

## Mission
Build an evidence-driven, self-improving AI crypto trading research and execution system.

## Development progression

`$0 capital → education → research → backtesting → robustness → paper trading → controlled automation → potentially small live trading → controlled scaling`

## V0 objective
Establish the smallest useful research foundation before introducing historical data pipelines, paper trading, execution, risk infrastructure, or AI agents.

## Core principle
**EDGE → DATA → RULES → TEST → VALIDATE → PAPER TRADE → EXECUTE**

A positive backtest is evidence about a specified historical simulation, not proof of a durable edge.
