# Project

## Mission
Build an evidence-driven, self-improving AI crypto trading research and execution system.

## Development progression

`$0 capital → education → research → backtesting → robustness → paper trading → controlled automation → potentially small live trading → controlled scaling`

## V0 objective
Establish the smallest useful research foundation before introducing historical data pipelines, paper trading, execution, risk infrastructure, or AI agents.

## Core principle
**MARKET THEORY → HYPOTHESIS → DATA → RULES → TEST → VALIDATE → LOCKED OOS → PAPER TRADE → CONTROLLED DEPLOYMENT → MONITOR EDGE → ADAPT / RETIRE → NEW HYPOTHESIS**

Adaptation means versioned research and governance, not unrestricted automatic self-modification.

A positive backtest is evidence about a specified historical simulation, not proof of a durable edge.

The project treats strategy edges as potentially conditional and temporary. Strategies that later degrade are reviewed against predefined criteria; failure is preserved as research evidence rather than automatically optimized away.
